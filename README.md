# Luminara: Echoes of Balance

An original fantasy and science-fiction universe and tabletop roleplaying game.
Light and darkness are powerful forces, but the choices we make determine who we become.

## Play

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. Nothing to install.

### Round One: The Shattered Nexus

- Create a hero: a name, one of five ancestries (Human, Elf, Goblin, Tharrin, Sylari) and one of four houses (Luminaris, Stellara, Novara, Virellis). Each house adds +1 to a stat and gives a once-per-round power.
- **Balance Points** run from −10 (shadow) to +10 (light). Compassion and sacrifice pull toward light; selfishness and cruelty toward shadow.
- Risky choices are **d20 checks**: d20 + stat (+ item and house bonuses) against a difficulty. A natural 20 always succeeds; a natural 1 always fails.
- Scenes: the Lantern Market, the Concord Spire, the Chasm of Reflection, the Gauntlet of Echoes, the Bridge of Whispers and the heart of the Shattered Nexus.
- Six endings, shaped by your Balance, your relics and the people you saved.

Progress is saved in the browser.

## Setting notes

The founding story pairs Elara, a young witch, with Virek, a dark-sworn warlord who wields the Current, against the ancient entity Umbriox. To keep Luminara original, the game uses its own names in place of other franchises' terms (for example "the Current" and "the Veyl Dominion", and the Tharrin and Sylari ancestries).
